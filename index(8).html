FAQ

Before you order.

How do I order a CV?

Tap any WhatsApp button, tell us the job you are targeting and send your current CV if you have one.

What if I do not already have a CV?

You can send your work history, education, skills, certifications and target role. We will organize the verified information into a professional CV.

How do I pay?

Payment instructions are shared privately after your order and price are confirmed. Personal payment details are not published on this website.

Is every CV ATS-friendly?

The ATS Professional CV is designed with a simpler structure for online applications. More visual CVs can be useful in some situations but may not be ideal for every ATS.

Do you guarantee an interview or job?

No. A professional CV can improve presentation, but hiring decisions are made by employers and recruiters.

Will you add experience I do not have?

No. CVSouq does not invent employers, dates, qualifications, certifications or achievements.
